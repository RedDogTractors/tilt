Important Safety Notice

Tilt Trac is an operator aid that shows an approximate implement angle. It is not a safety device. Do not rely on it to prevent tip-over, rollover, loss of load, or any other hazard.

  • Displayed angles may be inaccurate or delayed because of sensor mounting, calibration, vibration, magnetic interference, Bluetooth signal loss, or phone or browser problems.
  • Always follow the operator's manuals and safety instructions for your tractor, loader and implements, and use your own judgment.
  • Do not look at or handle your phone while the tractor is moving. Mount the phone securely where it does not block your view or the controls.
  • Carry loads low, wear your seat belt with the ROPS raised, and keep bystanders clear.
